# Walleterm video design

Concept: a picture book for a careful tool. Flat cut-paper shapes on cream, one tomato mascot, and simple dark app panels that look like the real 1Password screens.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper | #fbf6e6 | Scene background |
| moon | #f4eddc | Large flat shapes |
| moss | #758747 | Offset panel shadow, accents |
| deep-moss | #2a4525 | Labels, key words |
| ink | #0a0a09 | Text on paper, panel outlines |
| tomato | #e74d36 | Mascot, callout rings, one focal accent for each scene |

Mock app panels use their own dark neutrals: #262626, #333333, #f2f2f2, #a3a3a3, with a blue #1a6dff action color and a purple #6f5fb5 banner.

## Type

- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, variable 200 to 800, for all text.
- Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, 400, for commands and labels.
- Headlines 76 px. Body 34 px. Panel text 28 px or larger. Labels 24 px.

## Layout

- Left column: step label, headline, one sentence. Right: one mock window with a solid moss offset shadow.
- Every scene has a tomato callout ring on the one thing to look at.
- Motion: panel slides in, callout rings draw, cursor moves to the target, then clicks.

## Do not

- No gradients, blur shadows, or neon.
- No real account data.
- No 1Password logo. Use a plain blue circle with a keyhole.
