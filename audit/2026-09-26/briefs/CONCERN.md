# Independent concern review

Read `COMMON.md`. This assignment overrides its initial independence and research allocation rules.
The parent assigns one concern identifier, one model, and one report path.
Read the named original reports, source paths, reproduction scripts, and logs.
Do not read the paired concern review before submitting your own conclusion.
Other concern reviews are outside your assignment.

Decide whether the concern is confirmed, conditional, an accepted limit, or unsupported.
Test reachability against the actual macOS, Bun, browser, testnet, and 1Password boundaries.
Separate a mocked failing condition from a demonstrated production path.
Do not infer key theft, unauthorized signing, submission, or public exposure without direct evidence.
Check the smallest mitigation against new complexity and changed trust assumptions.
Reject irrelevant hardening recommendations and unsupported severity inflation.

Provide a compact report with these fields:

- Concern identifier, model, effort, baseline, and assigned source scope.
- Verdict, priority, confidence, and actual affected users.
- Reachable scenario and exact source references.
- Evidence and counterevidence, including checks you actually ran.
- Minimum mitigation, alternatives, and verification steps.
- Primary sources, research usage, and remaining limits.

Aim for 50–100 lines. Stop when the disputed question has evidence.
Use 25–50 lines when direct evidence rejects the concern's premise.
Do not implement fixes or repeat the full baseline suite.
Use isolated local mocks only. Never request live signatures or open public tunnels.

All requested research tools remain available under `COMMON.md`.
Reuse preserved primary-source evidence when it answers the question.
Use Raven, Jev, Parallel MCP, parallel-cli, or Perplexity for unresolved factual questions.
The total follow-up allocation is $1 per reviewer, including at most $0.25 for Jev.
An already assigned higher allocation remains valid for that active reviewer only.
Do not repeat searches solely to increase a tool count.
Record failed or partial provider results and unknown charges.
